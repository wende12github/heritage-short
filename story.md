# Story: ተስፋ አለመቁረጥ (Never Give Up)

A short fictionalised story inspired by the encouragement friends and family give when repeated failures make us want to quit.

- **Character:** Yonas (fictional young Ethiopian man in his early twenties, `real_person: false`).
- **Setting:** A small study/work room in Addis Ababa, from a rainy night to sunrise.
- **Plot (9 beats):**
  1. Late at night, rain outside, Yonas sits at a desk under a warm lamp where nothing has gone as he hoped.
  2. No matter how hard he tries, his sketches and notes keep failing; he crumples another page beside cold tea.
  3. Exhausted and discouraged, he closes his notebook and rubs his eyes, slowly losing hope.
  4. Looking at his phone on the table, he tells his closest friend: "I don't want to try anymore. Nothing works out for me."
  5. His friend listens patiently (kept off-screen / remembered voice so only one character appears on screen).
  6. His friend reminds him: "Success is not always winning; sometimes falling is life's lesson."
  7. As the first blue-gold light of dawn touches the curtains: "Even if nothing worked today, tomorrow can bring a new chance. You are stronger than you think."
  8. Yonas smiles slightly, opens a fresh page, and picks up his pen to try again.
  9. Morning sunlight fills the room: failure is not the end of the journey—as long as there is hope, there is a new beginning.
